How it works

Four simple steps

Each chapter is broken into small ideas you can finish on the bus, before dinner or just before a test.

  1. 1Swipe idea cards

    Every chapter becomes short, illustrated cards. Read one, swipe to the next.

  2. 2Save with the heart

    Tap the heart on any card to keep it for quick revision later.

  3. 3Flashcards and a quick quiz

    Finished the ideas? Check what stuck with flashcards, then a short quiz.

  4. 4My Diary

    Keep the timetable, homework and upcoming tests in one place.

For parents

Made to be safe and simple

Edubinge is built for school students, so we kept it calm and private.

  • No ads

    No banners, pop-ups or ad videos — nothing to tap by mistake.

  • No sign-in

    No account, email or password. Pick a class and start.

  • Works offline

    Lessons work without an internet connection.

  • Data stays on the phone

    Profiles, progress and diary entries never leave the device.

  • Several children, one phone

    Each child gets their own profile, class and progress.

Swipe. Discover. Understand. Repeat.
