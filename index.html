Edubinge

Learn your chapters one swipe at a time

Edubinge turns school chapters into short, illustrated cards that students swipe through, with flashcards, quick quizzes and a simple study diary. It works offline and has no ads.

Content follows the NCERT (CBSE) syllabus for Classes 7 to 10 and the NIOS Secondary course.
