Privacy policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

This policy explains how the Edubinge Android app ("the app") handles information. The app is published by Vaiva Technologies (Sujith Krishna), Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, India ("we").

The short version

  • We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information.
  • Everything you enter in the app stays on your phone.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no sign-in.

Information the app keeps on your device

To work, the app saves the following only in the app's own storage on your phone:

  • Learner profiles: the name or nickname you type, class and board.
  • Learning progress: chapters and cards viewed, saved cards, flashcard and quiz results, and recent activity.
  • Diary entries: timetable, homework, tests, to-dos, after-school activities and bag-packing ticks you add.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

Sharing that you choose

Some screens let you copy a weekly progress summary (ideas learned, quizzes, days active) to paste into another app such as WhatsApp. This happens only when you tap the button, and what you paste and where is your choice. Edubinge does not send it anywhere itself.

Children

Edubinge is made for school students, including children under 13. Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not collect any information from children. We encourage parents to set up profiles with their children. The app follows Google Play's Families policy, and contains no ads and no third-party tracking.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location or files.

Deleting your information

You can delete a learner profile inside the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings or uninstalling the app. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Purchases in the future

If we add paid content later, payments will be handled by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy. We will update this policy before any such change.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
